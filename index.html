Volleyball 101

Basic Rules

  • Each team has six players on the court.
  • A team can touch the ball a maximum of three times before hitting it over the net.
  • Players must cleanly strike the ball and cannot catch, hold, or throw it.
  • The first team to reach 25 points (and lead by at least 2) wins the set.
